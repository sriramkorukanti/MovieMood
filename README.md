# MovieMood
Pick tonight's film from your Letterboxd watchlist with an 8-question mood quiz.
Upload your own watchlist.csv (Letterboxd → Settings → Import & Export → Export your data)
Random pick and browse-all views
Posters via TMDB (add your own free API key in the Posters menu), with Wikipedia and Apple as keyless fallbacks

Everything runs in the browser. Your CSV and TMDB key never leave your device (only film titles and years are sent to TMDB).

Posters and film data via TMDB. This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.
